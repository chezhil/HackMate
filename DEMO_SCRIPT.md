# HackMate demo video script (under 2 minutes)

Record at 1440 × 900 or larger, signed in as `chezhil`, in light mode. Before you start, pin 3–4 upcoming
hackathons and open one hackathon once, so its rules are already read. The voice-over is about 260 words.

## 0:00–0:12 · The problem
**Show:** Discover. **Say:** "I enter a lot of hackathons, and the hard part isn't building, it's keeping track.
Events are spread over ten sites, deadlines clash, and the rules hide things like 'two-minute video' or 'solo
only'. So I built HackMate, entirely by voice with Wispr Flow."

## 0:12–0:32 · Discover
**Show:** Search "AI", pick In person and a city, switch to Split, click a map pin (the list jumps to it).
**Say:** "Discover pulls about 490 upcoming hackathons from ten sites into one list, with duplicates merged. I can
filter by format, city, prize and dates, and clicking a pin on the map takes me straight to it."

## 0:32–0:58 · Understand the rules
**Show:** Open a hackathon: the summary, the gotchas, then Ask AI ("Can I use code I wrote before?") and click a
citation. **Say:** "The AI has already read the full rules: what to submit, judging, team size, prizes. It flags
gotchas with the exact quote, and Ask AI answers only from the rules, with citations I can check."

## 0:58–1:20 · Plan it
**Show:** Pin it, open Tracker, drag a card to Building, point at the countdown, click Website, then Calendar and a
clash. **Say:** "Pinned hackathons land on my tracker with deadlines and countdowns. I drag them along as I
register, build and submit. The calendar warns me about clashes and emails me a day and three hours before."

## 1:20–1:40 · Build, pitch, submit
**Show:** Ideas, Pitch script, Mock judge Q&A, Submit checklist. **Say:** "It suggests ideas from the tracks and past
winners, writes a pitch timed to the judging criteria, grades my answers in a mock Q&A, and checks my submission
against this hackathon's own rules."

## 1:40–2:00 · After, and how it was built
**Show:** Results and Profile, then dictate one line with Wispr Flow into the coding agent, then the live URL.
**Say:** "Afterwards it keeps my results, prizes and certificates on a public profile. Every feature you saw was
built by describing it out loud with Wispr Flow. HackMate: hackmate-pied.vercel.app."

**Tips:** cut waiting (AI answers, loading) in editing; zoom in (Cmd +) on small text like citations.
