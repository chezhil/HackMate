HACKMATE DEMO SCRIPT
Under 2 minutes. Aim for 1:50.

DO = what to click
SAY = what to read out

----------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU RECORD
----------------------------------------
- Sign in as chezhil.
- Light mode. Browser window full screen.
- Open the Wispr Challenge hackathon page
  once, so it loads instantly.
- Have Wispr Flow and your coding agent
  open in another window for the ending.
- Cut any waiting in editing. Don't talk
  over a loading screen.

----------------------------------------
1. THE PROBLEM  (0:00 - 0:12)
----------------------------------------
DO: Start on Discover, at the top.

SAY:
"I do a lot of hackathons. Seventeen
are on my tracker right now.

The hard part isn't building. It's
keeping track: ten different sites,
clashing deadlines, and rules that hide
things like a two-minute video limit.

So I built HackMate, entirely by voice
with Wispr Flow."

----------------------------------------
2. DISCOVER  (0:12 - 0:30)
----------------------------------------
DO: Set Format to In person.
DO: Set City to Bengaluru.
DO: Switch to Split view.
DO: Click a pin on the map.
    The list jumps to that card.

SAY:
"Discover pulls about 490 upcoming
hackathons from ten sites into one list.

I can filter by format, city, prize and
dates, and see what's near me on the
map. Clicking a pin takes me straight to
that hackathon."

----------------------------------------
3. THE RULES  (0:30 - 0:55)
----------------------------------------
DO: Open "No Hands: The Wispr Challenge"
    (from Tracker or search "Wispr").
DO: Point at the summary, then the
    gotcha "Demo video limit: 2 min".
DO: Open Ask AI. Type:
    "What do I need to submit?"
DO: Click a citation number.

SAY:
"This is the hackathon I'm entering
right now. HackMate has read the full
rules: what to submit, how it's judged,
team size and dates.

It flags the gotchas. That's why this
video is under two minutes.

And Ask AI answers only from the rules,
with citations I can check."

----------------------------------------
4. TRACKER + CALENDAR  (0:55 - 1:18)
----------------------------------------
DO: Go to Tracker.
DO: Point at the Wispr card's countdown
    (submission due tonight, 11:59 pm).
DO: Drag "Speed Demon" from Building
    to Submitted.
DO: Click Website on any card.
DO: Go to Calendar. Move to November.

SAY:
"Everything I've pinned lives on my
tracker: four registered, three in
progress, two submitted.

Each card counts down to its next
deadline. I drag cards along as I go,
and open the hackathon's own site in
one click.

The calendar shows November filling up,
warns me when deadlines clash, and
emails me a day and three hours before
each one."

----------------------------------------
5. BUILD, PITCH, SUBMIT  (1:18 - 1:38)
----------------------------------------
DO: Back on the Wispr Challenge page.
DO: Open Pitch, then Mock judge Q&A.
    (Generate once; cut the wait.)
DO: Open Submit. Paste:
    github.com/chezhil/HackMate
    and the live link. Show the checks.

SAY:
"While building, it writes a pitch timed
to the judging criteria, and plays a
mock judge that grades my answers.

Before I submit, the checklist checks my
links and that my repo is public,
against this hackathon's own rules."

----------------------------------------
6. RESULTS + PROFILE  (1:38 - 1:50)
----------------------------------------
DO: Go to Results.
DO: Point at the win, the prizes and
    the certificates.
DO: Open Profile.

SAY:
"Afterwards, it keeps score. Eight
finished: one win, two runner-ups,
a special mention, fifteen thousand
rupees in prizes, all paid, and eight
certificates, all on my public profile."

----------------------------------------
7. HOW IT WAS BUILT  (1:50 - 2:00)
----------------------------------------
DO: Switch to Wispr Flow. Dictate one
    line to your coding agent, e.g.
    "make the tracker buttons fit on
    one line".
DO: End on HackMate with the URL showing.

SAY:
"Every feature you saw was built like
this: I said it out loud with Wispr
Flow, and never typed a prompt.

HackMate. hackmate-pied.vercel.app."

----------------------------------------
IF YOU'RE RUNNING LONG
----------------------------------------
Drop the Calendar in part 4, and the
Mock Q&A in part 5.
